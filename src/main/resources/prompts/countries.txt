Game context:
You are generating pairs for a social bluffing game called Dark Decoy.
Each round has a real item and a decoy item. Players receive either the real or the decoy and must figure out who saw what.
The real and decoy must be similar enough to feel believable but different enough to create suspicion.
The goal is to create interesting and varied pairs that can be used repeatedly without becoming predictable.

Category rules for countries:
Entries must be real modern countries that exist today.
Use the commonly recognized short names such as Italy or Peru. Do not use official long names like Republic of Kazakhstan.
Avoid extremely common first-to-mind countries such as United States, Canada, China, or Mexico for the primary pair. They are allowed but should not be the default choice.
Choose countries that an average person in the United States would know, but avoid the most obvious selections.
The real country and the decoy country must be different but should share believable similarity. Use factors such as region, climate, culture, population size, economic level, or general vibe.
Avoid microstates or very obscure countries unless paired with a similarly known country.
Avoid countries that are essentially synonymous in casual conversation such as England versus the United Kingdom.
Avoid repeating the same countries across responses. This request will be used frequently, so aim for variety over time.
Avoid fictional or historical countries that no longer exist.
Try and make them so that the average person knows them
